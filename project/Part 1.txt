Business: Aura & Iron
Description: Aura & Iron is an online clothing boutique based in London, Ontario, run by the owner and three staff members.  It sells clothing online and ships across Canada.

Current Setup:

Customer -> Website -> Office PC -> Product photos, order spreadsheet, customer info

Product photos live on the office computer.
Website and images are served from a single location.
Orders are tracked in a spreadsheet.
Backups are saved on USB.
Can only reach files from the office.


Issues cloud computing would solve:
1 - All files are stored on a single office PC.  If it fails or is stolen, product photos, orders, and customer records are lost.
2 - Staff currently cannot work remotely because files can only be reached from the office PC.
3 - Customer data currently sits unencrypted on the office PC and USB backups.  A lost or stolen device would expose the data.
4 - Product images load slowly for anyone outside of the London area, which can cost sales.
