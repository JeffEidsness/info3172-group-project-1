Part 5: Cloud Approach

Selection: Amazon Web services (AWS)

1) Business requirement: Current set up the files are stored on office PC.  If the PC goes down or stolen the information being tracked is gone
  
   Solution: AWS S3

   Provides A durable and available storage for data of all sizes, without the problem of provisioning capacity, as well as allowing centralized management of         continuous backups. With scalable and encrypted security for data management, while also allowing control access. 
            
2) Business requirement: The customers purchasing/checking out (cart service) 

   Solution: ElastiCache

   This will provide our ecommerce website with a cart service that's reliable. Delivers protection, as well as has fast and scalable results. Being able to           migrate as its an open source with little coding.
